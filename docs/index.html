Flash Jarvis 2.0

For the Waveshare ESP32-S3 1.85" round board. Nothing to install.

1

Plug the board into your Mac

Use a USB-C cable that does data. Use Chrome or Edge (Safari can't flash).

2

Click the button and pick the board

Choose the port that says USB JTAG/serial or usbmodem. Say yes to "erase" the first time.

This browser can't flash boards. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on your Mac.
Open this page over https to flash.
3

Set up Wi-Fi from your phone

Press RST on the board. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi Jarvis-Setup. A setup page opens (if not, go to 192.168.4.1). Pick your Wi-Fi, type the password, the Pi address and the Jarvis key (shown at the end of the Pi install). Leave the Pi address empty: the board finds Jarvis by itself.

4

Talk to Jarvis

Hold BOOT, talk, let go. Jarvis answers on your Nest. New Wi-Fi or Pi address later? Hold BOOT for 10 seconds and the setup hotspot opens again.

Flashing stuck or no port? Hold BOOT, press RST, let go of BOOT, then click Flash again.
